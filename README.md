# transformAItion

**An open, vendor-neutral guide to adopting AI tools in organisations.**

transformAItion helps any organisation, team or individual introduce AI tools responsibly and effectively. It covers the whole journey, from first questions to running AI tools at scale. Guidance is grounded in evidence, standards and regulation rather than opinion, and it is written so that you can apply it to your own context.

> This guide does not recommend any vendor. It helps you work out what you need, choose against your own criteria, and then implement whichever tools you choose well.

### The adoption lifecycle

Everything in the guide maps to six stages. You can enter at whichever stage you are at.

| Stage | Question it answers | Guide |
|---|---|---|

## How this guide works

- **Evidence first.** Claims that a reader could doubt are cited. Research findings carry an evidence-strength label. See the [editorial standards](CONTRIBUTING.md#editorial-standards).
- **Neutral.** The core guide names no preferred vendor. Tool guides describe, they don't promote.
- **Context-aware.** Guidance states when it applies, by organisation size, sector, jurisdiction and risk.
- **Dated.** Time-sensitive pages show when they were last reviewed.

## This is not legal advice

Content on regulation and data protection is summarised for orientation only. Take professional advice for decisions with legal consequences.
