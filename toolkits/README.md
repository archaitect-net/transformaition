# Toolkits

> **Who this is for:** Anyone who needs a ready-to-adapt document for AI adoption: policies, plans, business cases, role matrices, registers and libraries. Templates are designed for small organisations and enterprises alike.
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10

This section contains nine fill-in-the-blanks templates you can copy and adapt. Each has guidance notes in blockquotes explaining the choices you need to make, and [bracketed placeholders] for your content. Templates link to each other and to the [assessments](../assessments/README.md) so that the documents you produce form a coherent set. This page explains what each template is for, when in the [lifecycle](../docs/lifecycle/README.md) you need it, and how to adapt templates well.

## The templates

| Template | What it gives you | Main owner | Lifecycle stage | Effort to adapt |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| [AI policy](ai-policy-template.md) | Organisation-level policy: principles, roles, risk appetite, approval routes, lifecycle requirements | Senior sponsor / governance lead | [Assess](../docs/lifecycle/02-assess.md), formalised before [Scale](../docs/lifecycle/05-scale.md) | Medium |
| [Business case](business-case-template.md) | Full cost model, conservative benefits, sensitivity analysis, with a fictional worked example | Sponsor / programme lead / finance | [Select](../docs/lifecycle/03-select.md); refresh before [Scale](../docs/lifecycle/05-scale.md) | Medium–high |

## Which templates do I need first?

| Your situation | Start with |
|---|---|
| Staff are already using AI tools informally | [Acceptable use policy](acceptable-use-policy.md) and [use case register](use-case-register-template.md) (to record what exists) |
| You are about to run a first pilot | [Pilot plan](pilot-plan-template.md), [training plan](training-plan-template.md) (foundation module), [communication plan](communication-plan-template.md) (light version) |
| You need funding approval | [Business case](business-case-template.md) |
| Decisions are slow or nobody owns risk | [RACI](raci-template.md) and [AI policy](ai-policy-template.md) |
| You are scaling to many teams | [AI policy](ai-policy-template.md), [training plan](training-plan-template.md), [communication plan](communication-plan-template.md), [prompt library](prompt-library-template.md) |

**Small organisations:** a practical minimum is the acceptable use policy (with its one-page quick reference), the small-organisation RACI, a simple register, and the light versions of the training and communication plans. That can be done in a few days.

## How to adapt a template

1. **Copy it** into your own document system. These templates are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE), so you can adapt them freely with attribution.
2. **Read the guidance notes first.** They flag decisions you must make, not just blanks to fill.
3. **Replace every [placeholder].** Search for `[` before publishing.
4. **Delete the guidance blockquotes** from the final version.
5. **Check consistency** with your existing policies (information security, data protection, HR, procurement) and with the other templates you use. For example, data classification levels should be the same in the acceptable use policy, the prompt library and the register.
6. **Get the right reviews.** Policies typically need data protection, legal and HR review, and approval at the level set in your governance.
7. **Set a review date.** AI tools and regulation change quickly; six to twelve months is common for policies.

## What these templates are not

- **Not legal advice.** Templates that touch on regulation summarise it for orientation. Take professional advice for decisions with legal consequences.
- **Not a substitute for thinking about your context.** The templates are deliberately generic. A template adopted without tailoring tends to be ignored.
- **Not vendor-specific.** For configuring a specific product, see the [tool guides](../docs/tools/tool-guides/README.md).
