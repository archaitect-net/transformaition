# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

transformAItion is a shared resource for people of every background, role and level of technical experience. We want taking part to be respectful, welcoming and free from harassment for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when you disagree about evidence or approach.
- Assume good faith, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Critique content and sources, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and non-specialists. Plain-English questions are valuable.
- Disclose conflicts of interest, such as working for a vendor whose product is under discussion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or discriminatory language of any kind
- Personal attacks or insults
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Using the project to promote products or services in place of neutral contribution
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that breach this code, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors who repeatedly do so.

To report a concern, open a confidential report through GitHub's **Report content** feature or contact the maintainers via the organisation profile at [github.com/archaitect-net](https://github.com/archaitect-net). Reports will be handled promptly and discreetly.
