# Governance

> **Who this is for:** Leaders, governance leads, risk, compliance, legal, data protection and security professionals, and anyone setting rules for AI use.
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10

AI governance is the set of structures, roles, policies and processes that make sure AI tools are used in line with your organisation's objectives, values, risk appetite and legal obligations. This section explains how to set it up and what it must cover, from decision rights and principles through risk management, regulation, data protection, security and intellectual property. It is written to be proportionate: a small business needs a short policy and a named owner, while a regulated enterprise needs formal committees and documented controls. Nothing here is legal advice.

## Minimum viable governance

If you are starting from nothing, these six elements cover most of the ground for low and medium-risk use of AI tools:

1. **A named owner** accountable for AI use (in a small organisation, often a director).
2. **An acceptable use policy** that says which tools are approved, what data may be used, and how outputs must be checked. See the [acceptable use policy template](../../toolkits/acceptable-use-policy.md).
3. **A use case register** listing approved uses and their risk tier. See the [use case register template](../../toolkits/use-case-register-template.md).
4. **A proportionate assessment step** for new uses: screening, then deeper assessment for higher-risk uses. See [AI risk assessment](../../assessments/ai-risk-assessment.md).
5. **Supplier checks** before adopting a tool. See [vendor due diligence](../../assessments/vendor-due-diligence.md).
6. **Training** so people know the rules and the limits of the tools. This is also a legal duty for organisations within scope of the EU AI Act's AI literacy provision; see [regulation overview](regulation-overview.md).

Build from there as use grows in scale and risk. The [AI policy template](../../toolkits/ai-policy-template.md) and [RACI template](../../toolkits/raci-template.md) help formalise the next layer.
