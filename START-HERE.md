# Start here

> **Who this is for:** Anyone opening this guide for the first time
> **Last reviewed:** 2026-10

This page helps you find the right route through the guide in about two minutes. Answer three questions, then follow the link.

---

## Question 1: What is your role?

Your role decides your **pathway**: a curated reading route with the questions, decisions and resources that matter most to you.

| If you mostly… | Start with |
|---|---|
| Set direction, approve investment or own organisational risk | [Leadership](docs/pathways/leadership.md) |
| Lead a team and want it to use AI well | [Managers](docs/pathways/managers.md) |
| Run identity, devices, licensing, integration or platforms | [IT and platform](docs/pathways/it-and-platform.md) |
| Write software, or want to build with AI models | [Developers](docs/pathways/developers.md) |
| Work with data, analytics, BI or data governance | [Data teams](docs/pathways/data-teams.md) |
| Work in security, risk, legal, compliance, audit or data protection | [Risk and compliance](docs/pathways/risk-and-compliance.md) |
| Work in HR, people, change or learning and development | [People and learning](docs/pathways/people-and-learning.md) |
| Just want to use AI tools safely and effectively in your own job | [Everyday users](docs/pathways/everyday-users.md) |

Wearing several hats, which is common in small organisations? Start with **Leadership**, then read the **Risk and compliance** pathway's checklist.

---

## Question 2: Where is your organisation today?

Your answer decides which **lifecycle stage** to read first.

| Your situation | Read first |
|---|---|
| "We haven't really started. People are curious, or already using free tools informally." | [1. Discover](docs/lifecycle/01-discover.md) |
| "We have ideas, but we don't know whether we're ready or what the risks are." | [2. Assess](docs/lifecycle/02-assess.md) |
| "We know what we want to do and need to choose tools or use cases." | [3. Select](docs/lifecycle/03-select.md) |
| "We've chosen and need to test it properly." | [4. Pilot](docs/lifecycle/04-pilot.md) |
| "The pilot worked and we want to roll it out." | [5. Scale](docs/lifecycle/05-scale.md) |
| "It's live and we need to run, govern and improve it." | [6. Operate](docs/lifecycle/06-operate.md) |

Not sure? Take the [organisational readiness assessment](assessments/organisational-readiness.md). It takes about 30 minutes and tells you where to focus.

---

## Question 3: Have you already chosen your AI tools?

- **No.** Read [Choosing tools](docs/tools/choosing-tools.md). It's a requirements-led, vendor-neutral method.
- **Yes.** Go to the [tool guides](docs/tools/tool-guides/README.md) for implementation, configuration and data-control guidance for your chosen tool. If your tool isn't covered, use the [tool guide template](docs/tools/tool-guides/_template.md) as a checklist.

---

## If you only have one hour

1. Read [Capabilities and limitations](docs/foundations/capabilities-and-limitations.md) (15 minutes)
2. Skim the [Evidence on impact](docs/foundations/evidence-on-impact.md) summary table (10 minutes)
3. Adapt the [Acceptable use policy template](toolkits/acceptable-use-policy.md) for your organisation (35 minutes)

Those three steps put most organisations in a safer position than having no guidance at all.
